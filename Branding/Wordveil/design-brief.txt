WORDVEIL — BRAND DESIGN

English name: Wordveil
Meaning: Word + veil. Cover the word, recall it, then reveal the answer.
Tagline: Hide. Recall. Reveal.
Chinese companion line: 遮住答案，记住单词。

Identity
A bold W on an ivory study card, partially covered by a slate-blue ribbon with a lifted corner. The ribbon represents the app's cover-and-reveal interaction. The desaturated blue-gray palette relates to the in-app masks.

Production method
Native CoreGraphics and CoreText vector drawing. No image-generation CLI or API was used.
The built-in image generation service was attempted first but returned HTTP 404 without an image. The finished art was drawn from the design brief using native vector paths, not generated by that service.

Original submitted image-generation prompt (for future exploration)
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: final standalone iOS app icon artwork, square 1024 x 1024, for an original learning app named Wordveil.
Brand concept: Word + veil. The app lets people cover words on a photographed study page, recall the answer, and tap to reveal it. Calm, precise, tactile, sophisticated learning tool.
Create ONE polished icon, not a presentation board or mockup.
Composition: edge-to-edge square muted slate-blue background (#7C8FA6 family), fully opaque with no rounded outer corners and no external margin. Center one thick ivory study card with gently rounded corners, front-facing with only a slight dimensional relief, occupying about 64% of the canvas width and 69% of its height. Balanced generous safe margins. On the card is a large custom dark slate uppercase W. A broad opaque lighter blue-gray masking ribbon crosses the lower-middle portion of the W, hiding part of the letter. The ribbon has a single small curled/lifted right end, with a warm ivory underside, suggesting a cover being peeled back to reveal the word beneath. This card, W, and lifted ribbon must read as ONE compact recognizable symbol.
Style: beautifully restrained Apple-quality icon craft, simplified graphic silhouette, satin paper and matte ceramic feel, minimal soft bevels, subtle top-left light, crisp clean edges, controlled short contact shadows. Enough contrast and bold shapes to read at 48 pixels. Color is clearly blue-gray, not black. No glossy plastic, no glass, no sparkle, no decorative texture, no dramatic 3D angle.
Text: only the single letter W as part of the symbol. No app name, no tagline, no small text.
Constraints: original design, no familiar brand marks, no watermark, no phone/device mockup, no multiple icons, no transparent corners, no border around the entire canvas. Output the icon artwork itself.
